# Recomposition byte check (candidate)

`checkRecomposition({bytes, expectedBlocks, edits, pdfjs, exporter})` reads the output PDF with an injected PDF.js namespace. Supply `bytes` as a `Uint8Array`; `expectedBlocks` is the trusted, complete ordered logical text (`[{id,text}]`), and `edits` maps selected IDs to their exact requested After strings. The expectation must be established independently of the output destination map. Compare original to unedited output using original logical content, then unedited to edited output with the selected replacements. No map is accepted as evidence of content.

The checker compares the complete extracted text sequence, collapsing whitespace runs to one space. It does not remove word boundaries, normalize signs, repair hyphenation, or infer semantic equivalence. This tolerates ordinary line and page wraps at word boundaries; an extractor that splits a word across items or has different reading order can produce a finding requiring review. A mismatch includes expected and observed text, so results may contain document content and should be handled accordingly. Text matching is not a proof of visible ink or assets. Ordered substring locations are approximate observations, may be ambiguous with repeated content, and do not certify block ownership. Movement is reported separately and is not itself a failure.

Text item bounds are approximate and only flag clear page overflow. Rotated or unusual text may need separate review. Visual rendering, clipping, overlaps, figures, caption and asset ownership, styles and links are explicitly unavailable. Independent status is only a caller declaration that the named exporter is distinct from the PDF.js reader; it is not attestation of the actual exporter or an independent rendering check. The checker does not block download, and readable PDFs with findings remain usable. Unreadable input rejects rather than producing a success report.

`app/tests/recomposition-corpus.mjs` creates public synthetic PDFs using the pinned MuPDF vendor. The fixture corpus is handcrafted and does not establish real-paper extraction or round-trip quality. `node --test app/tests/recomposition-check.spec.mjs` exercises PDF.js against the generated bytes. No model, network, private paper, new package, or old quality semantics is involved.
