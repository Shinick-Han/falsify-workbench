# Recomposition byte check (candidate)

`checkRecomposition({bytes, expectedBlocks, edits, pdfjs, exporter})` reads the output PDF with an injected PDF.js namespace. Supply `bytes` as a `Uint8Array`; `expectedBlocks` is the trusted, complete ordered logical text (`[{id,text}]`), and `edits` maps selected IDs to their exact requested After strings. The expectation must be established independently of the output destination map. Compare original to unedited output using original logical content, then unedited to edited output with the selected replacements. No map is accepted as evidence of content.

The checker compares the complete extracted text sequence, collapsing whitespace runs to one space. Item strings are joined directly, preserving their spaces; EOL and clearly separate lines add a word boundary. For upright left-to-right items, a small adjacent gap joins runs and a clear larger gap adds a space. Intermediate gaps, significant overlaps, uncertain line alignment, or unsupported geometry make content unavailable rather than a pass. PDF.js may itself synthesize spaces or omit text, so the result is an extraction-based comparison, not an exact character or visibility proof. It does not remove word boundaries, normalize signs, repair hyphenation, or infer semantic equivalence. Expected After is used as supplied in the same logical whitespace comparison: an internal word space is not removed. A mismatch includes expected and observed text, so results may contain document content and should be handled accordingly. Ordered substring locations are approximate observations, may be ambiguous with repeated content, and do not certify block ownership. Movement is reported separately and is not itself a failure.

Bounds estimate upright left-to-right extracted items using PDF.js font ascent and descent relative to the baseline. Unsupported rotation, skew, page frame, missing metrics and nonfinite geometry are explicit; they cannot produce a successful bounds check. PDF.js may discard wholly off-page text or truncate it at the page edge before returning items, so even a checked result covers returned items only. The byte corpus tests bottom, top and right overflow where PDF.js returns text; the below-page baseline formula is also tested by changing a real extraction's geometry because PDF.js omits that item. Visual rendering, clipping, overlaps, figures, caption and asset ownership, styles and links are explicitly unavailable. Independent status is only a caller declaration that the named exporter is distinct from the PDF.js reader; it is not attestation of the actual exporter or an independent rendering check. The checker does not block download, and readable PDFs with findings remain usable. Unreadable input rejects rather than producing a success report.

`app/tests/recomposition-corpus.mjs` creates public synthetic PDFs using the pinned MuPDF vendor. The fixture corpus is handcrafted and does not establish real-paper extraction or round-trip quality. `node --test app/tests/recomposition-check.spec.mjs` exercises PDF.js against the generated bytes. No model, network, private paper, new package, or old quality semantics is involved.
