
The `layoutQuality` field is additive to `falsify.recomposition-check.v1`; the existing content and unavailable visual checks retain their meaning. It is diagnostic and never controls download. `inspectLayoutQuality({bytes, mupdf, profile})` can also score candidates directly. `score` is 100 minus the sum of explicit issue penalties, floored at zero; it only compares detected defects under the same profile and is not a visual pass. Issue boxes are `[left, top, right, bottom]` in zero-origin MuPDF display points and page indices are zero-based. Each issue includes its evidence and penalty. The report is partial even with no findings.

Optional `layoutProfile` contains `bodyFontSize` in points, `bodyFontNames` (MuPDF names), `captions` entries `{text, assetPageIndex, assetBbox}`, `headings` entries `{text}`, and `columns` entries `{pageIndex, bbox}`. Candidate caption text must match exactly one observed line and its candidate box must match exactly one observed image; metadata alone establishes nothing. Text inside an image is allowed as a label. Font comparisons are heuristics for sufficiently long text lines and may include non-body text; inspect findings before repair. A nominated empty column is a heuristic and does not account for vector-only content.

Painted text bounds can expose ink outside the page; they cannot prove whether an in-page clip path cuts ink. Text paint operations supply overlap candidates, and image placements come from structured text. Small glyph touching, containment, complex writing directions, multi-line captions, vector figures, clipping, transparency, layers, occlusion and actual ownership are not completely verified. The PDF may omit clipped material from structured text. A zero penalty does not imply any unsupported condition passed. Geometry limits make the whole report unavailable, not a fabricated pass. No raster or external model is used. LayoutFlow was not copied: its object proposal metrics do not establish actual ink, style or caption ownership. Source models and existing MuPDF 1.28.1 APIs are reused.
